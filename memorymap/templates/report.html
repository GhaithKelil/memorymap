Method and limits

MemoryMap enumerated the target's address space with VirtualQueryEx and read committed, readable pages with ReadProcessMemory. Memory is reduced to ASCII and UTF-16LE strings and matched against validated patterns (checksums for card numbers and Bitcoin addresses, structure checks for JWTs). Read-only file-backed pages are skipped because they match what is on disk. Anomaly checks look at page permissions, backing files, PE structure, entropy and offensive-tool strings. Findings describe what was present in memory at scan time; they do not prove compromise. JIT compilers and managed runtimes legitimately create executable, unbacked memory. Secrets in memory that is paged out, in other processes, or in the kernel are not covered.
